Music

Double Bass — Jazz Performance

I play double bass — the instrument that sits at the foundation of the jazz rhythm section, bridging harmony and pulse. My playing is rooted in the jazz tradition, informed by years of listening, practice, and performance.

I perform primarily in two settings: small combos — trio, quartet, and quintet configurations where the bass carries both the harmonic bottom and a direct conversational role — and big bands, where precision, blend, and sectional discipline define the craft.

Whether walking a blues in B♭, anchoring a lush ballad, or driving a uptempo bebop head, I'm committed to serving the music: listening first, supporting the soloist, and finding the pocket.
